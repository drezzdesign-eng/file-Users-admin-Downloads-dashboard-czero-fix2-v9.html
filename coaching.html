Staff Information Maklumat Staf

Instructions Arahan

  1. Listed are competencies that impact on performance. Berikut disenaraikan kompetensi yang memberi kesan kepada prestasi.
  2. Each competency is described in 5 levels of achievement. Discuss and agree on the best decision based on consistent behaviour displayed. Setiap kompetensi diterangkan dalam 5 tahap pencapaian. Bincang dan setuju keputusan terbaik berdasarkan tingkah laku konsisten yang ditunjukkan.
  3. Where disagreements arise, the coach's assessment of the current level of competence will prevail. Sekiranya berlaku percanggahan pendapat, penilaian coach terhadap tahap kompetensi semasa akan diguna pakai.
  4. Leave blank / state N/A where the competency is not applicable. Biarkan kosong / nyatakan N/A jika kompetensi tersebut tidak berkaitan.
  5. Each level has a range of 3 degrees (e.g. Excellent = 13, 14 or 15) — pick the score that best fits. Setiap tahap mempunyai julat 3 darjah (cth: Cemerlang = 13, 14 atau 15) — pilih markah yang paling sesuai.
Applicable to all staff — assess based on behaviour relevant to each staff's own job scope. Terpakai untuk semua staf — nilai berdasarkan tingkah laku yang berkaitan dengan skop kerja masing-masing.
